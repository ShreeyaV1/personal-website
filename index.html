About

Hi, I'm Shreeya. I like ideas that take root.

I'm an incoming Honours Mathematics student at the University of Waterloo. Most of what I've built started with a community I care about.

Lately I've been building Face Control, a cursor you move with your head, and worked with a team on QuBench, quantum-fair jury selection for IBM Qiskit Fall Fest. Before that, I led a mentor-matching platform for newcomers in Calgary, inspired by my own experience of finding my feet in a new place. I've taught coding, reading and math to young learners at the Calgary Public Library, and helped run an international youth advisory board spanning Canada, the U.S. and Uganda. I also write Python, C++ and Racket, and build 3D scenes in Autodesk Maya.
